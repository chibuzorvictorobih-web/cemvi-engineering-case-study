# Sources — cooling COP
ASHRAE Handbook, Chapter 2, “Thermodynamics and Refrigeration Cycles,” Section 1.5. Used for the cooling coefficient of performance (COP), defined as cooling provided divided by energy input. https://handbook.ashrae.org/Handbooks/F21/IP/F21_Ch02/F21_Ch02_ip.aspx

The example COP of 3.0 is my invented assumption; ASHRAE did not supply it.
