# CEMVI vibration-model sources

## MIT OpenCourseWare: Forced vibration

- **Authors:** Thomas Peacock and Nicolas Hadjiconstantinou
- **Title:** *Dynamics and Control I*, Lecture 21: “Vibrations: Second Order Systems – Forced Response”
- **Publisher:** Massachusetts Institute of Technology, MIT OpenCourseWare
- **Year:** 2007
- **Link:** https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/2-003j-dynamics-and-control-i-spring-2007/37024ed53f13e1710f2c5d193be95774_lec21.pdf

### What I used

- **PDF page 1:** The mass–spring–damper equation, m ẍ + c ẋ + kx = F(t).
- **PDF page 4:** The steady-state movement amplitude and phase when the force repeats periodically. I used this to calculate and plot the fictional steel-arm movement.

### What the source does not provide

The 10 kg mass, 10 Hz frequency, 0.10 damping ratio, 5 N peak force, fan-and-motor example, and link between cooling load and force amplitude are **my invented educational assumptions**. The arm stiffness values come from my earlier calculations.
