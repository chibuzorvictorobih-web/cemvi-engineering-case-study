# Sensitivity sources
## Cooling coefficient of performance
- Source: ASHRAE Handbook, Chapter 2, “Thermodynamics and Refrigeration Cycles,” Section 1.5.
- Link: https://handbook.ashrae.org/Handbooks/F21/IP/F21_Ch02/F21_Ch02_ip.aspx
- Used for: COP as cooling provided divided by the energy input required by a refrigeration cycle. I rearranged this relationship to calculate cooling load divided by COP.
- CEMVI's specific COP values, fan power, pump power, and 24-hour loads are illustrative project inputs, not values supplied by ASHRAE.
