# CEMVI source log
1.	U.S. Department of Energy, Building Energy Modeling 101. Used to understand how room heat gains relate to cooling load and how circulated water and coils can cool air. Numerical values borrowed: none. www.energy.gov
2.	U.S. Department of Energy, Operations & Maintenance Best Practices Guide, §9.4. Used for the basic evaporator–compressor–condenser–expansion cycle. Its condenser-water illustration is not our chosen air-cooled arrangement. Numerical values borrowed: none. www1.eere.energy.gov
3.	Carrier, 30XV Air-Cooled Chiller Technical Specifications. Used to confirm that an air-cooled chiller using R-513A is a publicly documented configuration. We are not modelling that product or using its ratings. Numerical values borrowed: none. www.carrier.com
Additional public sources for the heat-balance equation, cooling COP, material properties, beam calculations, and vibration model are recorded in the other files in this folder. Invented inputs and their boundaries are recorded in `assumptions.md/`.
