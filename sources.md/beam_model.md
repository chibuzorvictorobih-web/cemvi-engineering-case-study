# Source for the support-arm bending model
MIT OpenCourseWare, *3.032 Mechanical Behavior of Materials,
Problem Set 1 Solutions*, page 1. The clamped-free beam example gives:

- Rectangular second moment of area: I = b × h³ / 12
- Tip deflection under an end force: deflection = F × L³ / (3 × E × I)

## Bending-stress formula

Fairfield University, *MEEG3311 Machine Design Notes 02*,
PDF page 17, slide 33. For a cantilever with a force at its
free end, the maximum moment is M = F × L. The maximum nominal
bending stress is stress = M × c / I, at the fixed end's
top and bottom surfaces.

https://www.faculty.fairfield.edu/wdornfeld/ME311/MEEG3311MachineDesignNotes02.pdf
