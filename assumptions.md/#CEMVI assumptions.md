# CEMVI assumptions

- The equipment room and all its heat values are fictional.
- Equipment A is assumed to give off more heat during daytime.
- Equipment B follows an opposite fictional schedule.
- Solar heat is zero at night and rises during the invented daylight hours.
- Each time in the table marks the start of a one-hour period.
- Total heat equals Equipment A heat + Equipment B heat + solar heat.
- In our first simple model, we assume the room stays at its target temperature and does not store heat. Under that assumption, the heat entering during an hour is the cooling required during that hour.
- The three heat columns and their totals are in kW of heat, not kW of chiller electricity.
- The chiller is air-cooled. There is no condenser-water loop or cooling tower.
- No inputs come from an employer or a real installation.
